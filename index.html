Carrel

Privacy policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Carrel is a focus timer with an AI study partner. Almost everything it knows about you stays on your phone, and nothing is sent to the AI unless you allow it.

Who makes Carrel

Carrel is made by Brendan Song (“we”). If you have a question about this policy or your information, email brendan_song@icloud.com.

What stays on your phone

Carrel saves the following only on your device, and we never receive it:

  • your sessions, times, streaks, levels and watts
  • your camp: campmates, items, skies, hearths and where you placed them
  • what you said you’re working on, and the notes written after your sessions
  • your settings, such as sound and the study partner switch

Carrel has no accounts and no sign-in. Conversations with the study partner aren’t saved, even on your phone.

What goes to the AI, and only with your permission

The study partner is Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. The first time you try to use it, Carrel asks for your permission. If you allow it, Carrel sends information only when you use the study partner: when you ask a question, tap a quick button such as “Quiz me,” share a photo, or finish a session (for the notes written afterward). What it sends:

  • the text you type
  • any photo you choose to share
  • what you said you’re working on, and how long the session ran
  • the recent conversation, so replies make sense

This is used only to answer you and to write your session notes. Requests pass through a small relay server we run on Cloudflare, which forwards them to Anthropic and keeps no copy of your conversations. Like any web service, Cloudflare may briefly process technical details such as your IP address in order to deliver the request. Anthropic handles the information under its own terms; see Anthropic’s privacy policy.

You can switch the study partner off at any time in the You tab. The timer, the camp and everything else keep working.

Photos and camera

Carrel uses your camera or photo library only when you tap the camera button in the chat, and only the photo you pick leaves your phone. iPhone asks for your permission first, and you can change it in Settings.

Purchases

Carrel Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat, a subscription service, to confirm whether your subscription is active. It receives purchase records from Apple tied to an anonymous identifier, not your name or payment information.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads.
  • We don’t sell or rent your information.
  • We don’t track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don’t use analytics to monitor how you use Carrel.

Children

Carrel isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has shared information through the study partner, email us and we’ll help.

Keeping it safe

Everything Carrel sends travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Deleting your information

Deleting Carrel from your phone removes everything it stored there. Because we don’t keep your conversations, there’s nothing held on our side to erase, but you’re welcome to email us with any request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
